Overview

Smart Training Institute is a complete online training platform where users can explore courses and training packages, create an account, add their selections to a cart, and purchase them online. The platform also includes an administration area for managing the institute’s courses, instructors, users, purchases, and schedules.

The project was designed to provide a realistic online learning experience while giving the institute an easy way to manage its content and day-to-day operations.

Project Focus

  • Created an online catalog for courses and training packages.
  • Added user accounts, shopping cart, and online purchasing features.
  • Built a weekly schedule for available courses.
  • Created an admin area for managing courses, packages, instructors, users, and purchases.
  • Designed separate experiences for students and administrators.
  • Added account and purchase management to make the platform practical for a real training institute.

The project strengthened my understanding of building complete web applications, managing user interactions and information, and creating a platform that serves both customers and administrators.
